# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview
This project presents an **end-to-end analysis of Superstore sales data**, focusing on sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, shipping preferences, and regional trends.  
The objective is to generate **actionable business insights** that support **strategic planning, inventory optimization, and marketing decisions**.

The analysis is delivered through **interactive Power BI dashboards**, including a **short-term 15-day sales forecast** to enable data-driven planning.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze overall **sales, profit, and order volume**
- Identify **top-performing categories and sub-categories**
- Understand **customer segments and payment behavior**
- Evaluate **regional and state-level performance**
- Assess **shipping preferences and delivery efficiency**
- Support decision-making using **short-term sales forecasting**

---

## 🖼 Dashboard Overview

### 1️⃣ Sales Performance Dashboard
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business KPIs and trends:
- **KPIs:** Total Sales, Profit, Quantity Sold, Average Delivery Time  
- **Sales & Profit Trends:** Month-over-month comparison across years  
- **Category & Sub-Category Analysis:** Identification of top revenue contributors  
- **Customer Segments:** Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office contribution  
- **Shipping & Payment Modes:** Insights into customer preferences  
- **Geographic Analysis:** State-wise sales and profit distribution  

---

### 2️⃣ Sales Forecast Dashboard (15 Days)
This dashboard focuses on **time-series analysis and forecasting**:
- Historical sales trends by order date  
- **15-day sales forecast** using Power BI forecasting capabilities  
- Identification of **short-term demand patterns**  
- State-wise sales ranking to highlight high-performing regions  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Category Performance:**  
  Office Supplies and Technology are the highest revenue-generating categories, with **Phones and Chairs** emerging as top-performing sub-categories.

- **Regional Trends:**  
  **California** leads overall sales, followed by New York and Texas, indicating strong regional demand and opportunities for targeted marketing.

- **Customer Segments:**  
  The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of total sales, highlighting the importance of consumer-focused strategies.

- **Shipping Preferences:**  
  **Standard Shipping** is the most preferred mode, suggesting customer preference for cost-effective delivery and scope for logistics optimization.

- **Payment Behavior:**  
  Online and COD transactions account for a significant portion of sales, reinforcing the need for reliable and optimized digital payment systems.

- **Seasonality:**  
  Sales and profit show noticeable spikes toward the **end of the year**, presenting strong opportunities for promotional and inventory planning.

- **Sales Forecasting:**  
  Short-term forecasting indicates **stable sales performance over the next 15 days**, supporting proactive planning around inventory and promotions.
